`setup-beam` is a GitHub Actions action maintained by the [Erlang Ecosystem Foundation](https://erlef.org) that installs and configures the full BEAM toolchain — Erlang/OTP, Elixir, Gleam, and rebar3 — in your CI pipeline. It supports Ubuntu, Windows, and macOS runners, flexible version strategies, and integrates problem matchers for clearer build output.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the action to your workflow">
    Create or open `.github/workflows/ci.yml` in your repository.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the versions you need">
    Use `otp-version`, `elixir-version`, `gleam-version`, or `rebar3-version` inputs under `with:`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run your test suite">
    Call `mix test`, `rebar3 ct`, or `gleam test` immediately after the setup step — the tools are on `PATH`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

```yaml .github/workflows/ci.yml
on: push

jobs:
  test:
    runs-on: ubuntu-24.04
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@v4
      - uses: erlef/setup-beam@v1
        with:
          otp-version: '27'
          elixir-version: '1.18'
      - run: mix deps.get
      - run: mix test
```

<Note>
  Specify versions as YAML strings (quoted), not numbers. `otp-version: '26.2'` and `otp-version: 26.2` are not the same — the unquoted form may be truncated by YAML parsers.
</Note>
